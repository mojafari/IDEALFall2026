# Activity 08: AI Drone Challenge

## Goal

Use AI as a programming partner while demonstrating that you understand and can verify the resulting program.

----

## Choose One Challenge

### Challenge A: Triangle

Modify the square flight program so that the drone flies a triangle.

Requirements:

* 3 sides
* 100 cm per side
* correct turn angle
* takeoff
* landing
* use a loop

----

### Challenge B: Pentagon

Modify the program so that the drone flies a pentagon.

Requirements:

* 5 sides
* 100 cm per side
* calculate the turn angle
* use a loop
* explain the geometry

----

### Challenge C: Hexagon

Modify the program so that the drone flies a hexagon.

Requirements:

* 6 sides
* 75 cm per side
* use a loop
* use variables for side length and number of sides

----

## Required AI Interaction

You must use AI at least three times.

### Interaction 1: Explanation

Ask AI to explain your starting program.

Save the prompt and response.

----

### Interaction 2: Modification

Ask AI to help you modify the program.

Save the prompt and response.

----

### Interaction 3: Review

Ask AI to review your final program.

Use:

````text
Review this program.

Do not rewrite it.

Instead:

1. Identify possible programming errors.
2. Identify possible logic errors.
3. Identify anything that could be unsafe.
4. Identify anything I should test.
5. Explain why you identified each issue.
````

Save the prompt and response.

----

## Human Verification

Before testing the program, answer:

````text
Number of sides:
________________

Movement distance:
________________

Turn angle:
________________

Number of loop repetitions:
________________
````

Explain why each answer is correct.

### 4. Your Evaluation
Write 150–250 words answering:

````text
What did AI do well?

What did AI get wrong?

What did I have to verify?

What did I learn about prompting?

What did I learn about programming?

Would I trust AI-generated code without reviewing it?
Why or why not?
````

----

## Important

Your grade is not based only on whether the drone successfully flies.

You should be able to explain:

* What your code does.
* Why your code works.
* What AI contributed.
* What you changed.
* What you verified.
* What could go wrong.
